About Us

Marshall Area Stage Co. is a community theatre organization formed in 1999. In cooperation with Marshall Community Services, and with the assistance of Marshall Area Fine Arts Council, (MAFAC), we hope to continue providing annual productions and theatre workshops while supporting other art functions in our community. We are proud to enhance the cultural environment this area offers.

OUR MISSION - To encourage the growth of the performing arts in Marshall & surrounding communities.

OUR GOAL - To enrich, entertain and educate Southwest Minnesota!
